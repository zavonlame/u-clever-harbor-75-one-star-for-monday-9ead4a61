A one-star review of Monday, written as though Monday were a restaurant.
22 words, 132 characters, one line — the way a weary office worker would
drop it into the group chat.

> One star. Arrived Monday, left Monday. Food never came, the coffee tasted like deadlines, and the waiter charged me for the weekend.

`main.ts` (Node 22, stdlib only) writes the review to
`$MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR/entry.txt`.
